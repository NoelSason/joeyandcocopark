Joey & Coco

A loving tribute to our best friends

This space is dedicated to Joey (Jojo) and Coco. They have brought so much character, chaos, and love into our lives. Even with their health struggles, their unique personalities shine bright.

Coco

6 Years Old Brown 23 lbs

The sweet, cuddly, and wonderfully round one. Coco might not be the sharpest tool in the shed, but he makes up for it with a heart of absolute gold and boundless friendliness. Weighing in at a delightfully chunky 23 pounds, he lives for two things: snuggles and food—especially if it belongs to his brother, Joey. Even while bravely battling Type II diabetes, his goofy, sweet-natured spirit never wavers. He is a boy who knows exactly what he wants, and what he definitely does not want is to go for a walk.

  • Will happily steal Joey's dinner right from under his nose.
  • Considers "going for a walk" to be a cruel and unusual punishment.
  • Endlessly friendly, undeniably goofy, and deeply loved.

Joey (Jojo)

7 Years Old White

The smart, fiercely loyal, and selective one. Jojo packs a tremendous amount of intelligence and personality into his small, slender frame. Battling Type I diabetes has been tough on his weight, but it hasn't dulled his sharp mind. He isn't exactly the friendliest guy on the block to strangers—in fact, he's known for his hilarious and bizarrely specific quirk of only trusting Asian people! He's a complex, brilliant little dog who needs frequent potty breaks every two hours, but the fierce loyalty he shows to his chosen humans makes every challenge worth it.

  • Incredibly smart, though he reserves his affection for a very select few.
  • Has a famously funny and highly specific preference for who he trusts.
  • Requires potty breaks every two hours (a brave side effect of his Type I diabetes).

Precious Memories

Forever in our hearts, Joey and Coco.
